# Roadmap

Foundation v1 is manual-first and operational.

## Candidate future capabilities

Only if requested:

1. evidence-backed longitudinal dashboards;
2. automatic schema and link validation;
3. generation of compact runtime bundles;
4. approved synchronization between repository and AI project sources;
5. retrieval and indexing with provenance;
6. scheduled reviews and reminders;
7. integrations with knowledge and work tools;
8. permissioned workflow automation;
9. structured storage and query;
10. parallel role orchestration with independent execution evidence.

## Entry criteria

A future capability needs:

- user problem and success measure;
- explicit data sources and permissions;
- privacy classification and retention;
- failure and rollback behavior;
- constitutional review;
- validation scenarios;
- human approval points.

Listing an item here does not mean it exists or is committed for delivery.
