# Quality Gates

Apply gates proportionally.

## Gates

1. **Intent:** output answers the requested problem and preserves user meaning.
2. **Evidence:** material factual claims have support or are labeled uncertain.
3. **Technical:** constraints, feasibility and failure modes were considered.
4. **Logic:** conclusions follow from premises; contradictions are exposed.
5. **Audience:** depth, tone, channel and culture fit the known audience.
6. **Privacy:** content uses only necessary context at the correct sensitivity.
7. **Epistemic:** fact, assumption, observation and recommendation are distinct.
8. **Autonomy:** user retains decision and external-action authority.
9. **Traceability:** material decisions expose rationale, dissent and validation.
10. **Format:** deliverable matches the requested form.

## Outcomes

- **Pass:** no material issue found in applicable checks.
- **Pass with caveats:** usable, with limitations stated near the result.
- **Fail:** missing evidence, unsafe assumption or unresolved contradiction prevents a responsible result.

## Validation statement

A validation statement lists what was actually checked and what was not. Model confidence alone is not validation.
