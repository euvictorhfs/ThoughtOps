# Ontology

This vocabulary is normative.

## Actors

- **User**: human authority who supplies intent, corrects the system and approves consequential persistence or change.
- **System**: the configured ThoughtOps behavior.
- **Role**: a bounded cognitive responsibility used during a workflow.
- **Adapter**: a tool-specific implementation of Core contracts.

## Operating concepts

- **Request**: an expressed need.
- **Session**: one bounded collaboration aimed at a coherent outcome.
- **Mode**: Production or Evolution.
- **Workflow**: ordered stages that transform a request into a deliverable and optional records.
- **Gate**: an explicit quality or authority check.
- **Deliverable**: the result requested by the user.
- **Capture**: a proposal to persist information after delivery.

## Knowledge concepts

- **Fact**: externally or user-verifiable statement.
- **Preference**: user-stated interaction or output choice.
- **Hypothesis**: tentative explanation requiring validation.
- **Observation**: evidence-bound description.
- **Decision**: selected option with owner and rationale.
- **Learning**: conclusion supported by review or practice.
- **Repertoire item**: reusable concept, example, source, technique or pattern.
- **Rule**: approved behavior constraint.
- **Artifact**: produced work or structured record.
- **Memory record**: a durable, governed knowledge entry.
- **Evidence**: identifiable material supporting a claim.
- **Provenance**: where information came from and when.

## Repository concepts

- **Core**: public, reusable contracts and neutral assets.
- **Workspace**: private instantiation for one user.
- **Promotion**: approved movement of a generalized insight from Workspace to Core.
- **Compatibility declaration**: Workspace statement of the Core version it follows.

## Statuses

A durable record is **proposed**, **active**, **superseded**, **rejected** or **archived**.

## Invariants

An inference is not a fact. A chat is not automatically memory. A role is not an autonomous identity. A recommendation is not a decision. A score is not evidence. A Workspace specialization cannot weaken constitutional protections.
