# Artifact Schemas

## Session brief

Required: outcome, audience, mode, context, constraints, confidentiality, success criteria. Optional: deadline, sources, format.

## Role output

Required: role, claim/recommendation, evidence, assumptions, confidence, risks, unresolved questions, next action.

## Synthesis

Required: outcome, rationale, alternatives, material dissent, assumptions, confidence, validation, user decision, capture proposal.

## Memory record

Required: ID, type, statement/content, provenance, scope, sensitivity, status, dates, relationships, approval. Confidence is required for hypotheses and observations.

## Decision record

Required: ID, date, owner, context, options, decision, rationale, trade-offs, evidence, consequences, review trigger.

## Observation

Required: ID, window, artifacts, rubric, pattern, counterexamples, interpretation, confidence, confounders, experiment, review date.

## ADR

Required: number, title, status, context, decision, alternatives, consequences, privacy/autonomy impact, compatibility, migration.

Templates instantiate these schemas. Adapters may serialize them differently without changing meaning.
